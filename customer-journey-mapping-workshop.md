# Customer Journey Mapping Workshop

## What to Deliver
A journey map covering: **Actor → Scenario → Phases → Actions/Emotions → Opportunities**

## 5 Questions (ask in order, adapt based on answers)

**Q1: Who is the actor?**
"Who are we mapping? A specific user persona, customer segment, or role?"
→ Get: name, job/context, key goals, biggest frustrations

**Q2: What scenario and goal?**
"What specific situation are we mapping? What is the actor trying to achieve?"
→ Get: trigger event, desired outcome, timeframe

**Q3: What are the journey phases?**
"What are the major stages from start to finish?"
→ Typical phases: Aware → Consider → Purchase → Onboard → Use → Renew/Exit
→ Adapt phases to the specific scenario

**Q4: For each phase — actions, thoughts, emotions**
- What does the actor DO? (touchpoints, channels)
- What are they THINKING? (questions, concerns)
- How do they FEEL? (frustration, delight, confusion) — rate 1–5

**Q5: Where are the biggest opportunities?**
"Where are the pain points? Where does the experience break down?"
→ Identify: pain points, moments of delight, gaps, contradictions
